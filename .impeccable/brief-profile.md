# Surface: public mechanic profile (`/mechanics/[slug]`), world shared by all Clutch surfaces

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a nervous car owner, usually on a phone, arriving from a text/social link, deciding whether to trust and contact this mechanic. Action: Request a quote (secondary: Share). Proof: identity + screening statuses, repair-category × make counts, provenance on every claim, verified rating with its base, repeat customers, price. Constraints: PRODUCT.md principles; never expose screening detail; self-reported visibly weaker; no trust score; no price ranking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A mechanic's profile is a numbered repair order, not a marketplace card. Each proven claim is a filled field inked in carbon blue with the copy that proves it named (Clutch copy, customer copy, employer copy, issuer copy); unproven fields stay visible as designed blanks. Refuses the category default: hero photo + stars + badge row + rounded booking card.

OWN-WORLD: Form-white ground #F4F5F1 (cool, never cream), ink #16181D, rule gray #B9BDC4 hairlines at 1px, carbon blue #3D3FC4 used only for verification (checkmarks, provenance marks, filled ticks), one canary #F2DA5C highlighter stroke reserved for the lines matching the visitor's car and repair, pencil gray #737985 with dashed rules for self-reported. Archivo variable only: condensed heavy (wdth ~75, wght 800) tabular numerals for counts, semi-condensed caps at small size for form field labels, normal width for body. Square corners (2px max), ruled boxes, numbered tickets, a tear-off stub. No gradients, no glass, no pills-everywhere, no emoji.

STORY: In ten seconds the visitor reads down one column: who (photo attached to the record, name), safe (Identity verified, Screened: background · driving · insurance as checked boxes), relevant (highlighted "18 brake jobs · 6 on BMW" when the link carries context), proven (34 verified repairs, 4.9 from 27 verified reviews, 12 repeat customers), price ($85/hr, $60 diagnostic). Every mark opens its copy. They request a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390: slim top rule with the Clutch wordmark left and Share right; photo print (84px square, white border, soft shadow) beside name at ~34px heavy semi-condensed and the work-model line; a two-cell ruled box of checked safety fields; the canary match line when context exists; a three-field ruled strip of heavy tabular counts; price field; sticky bottom stub with rate and "Request a quote" in ink black. Desktop: the same single form column (~720px) with a right tear-off stub (perforated left edge) carrying price, fees and the primary action, sticky.

FORM: The Repair Order — shop repair orders and multi-part carbon work forms; position 5 on my ordered list; seed key 2a54b624. Raises kept: designed blanks (seven-segment), one highlighter (zine), numerals carry the force (forge), one owned ink (single-hue), one axis (Versailles), photo as a real print (tropicália). Signature interaction: tapping any provenance mark opens "the copy" sheet naming who holds that copy, how it was verified, when, and until when; on contextual links the canary stroke draws once across matching ledger rows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
